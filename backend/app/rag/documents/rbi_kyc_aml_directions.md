# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Direction
## Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 (Updated AML/CFT)
### Section: Monitoring of Accounts and Prevention of Money Mule Networks

#### 1. Ongoing Due Diligence and Transaction Monitoring
- Regulated Entities (REs) shall pay special attention to all complex, unusually large transactions and all unusual patterns of transactions which have no apparent economic or visible lawful purpose.
- The background including all documents, purpose of transaction and origin of funds shall, as far as possible, be examined to determine whether the transaction carries high money laundering risk.

#### 2. Mule Account Identification Indicators
Banks must flag and freeze accounts exhibiting money mule characteristics, specifically:
- Freshly opened accounts or dormant accounts suddenly receiving large electronic transfers (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI).
- Immediate withdrawal or outward transfer ("rapid layering") of funds within minutes of receipt, leaving zero or negligible balance.
- Total discrepancy in account turnover compared to declared annual income or occupational profile.
- Multiple electronic transfers routed to accounts with zero previous transaction history.

#### 3. Step-up Verification and Freezing Protocols
- Where risk score exceeds the acceptable institutional risk appetite threshold, the bank must mandate Step-up Authentication (Time-based One-Time Password via out-of-band channel) or place the debit instruction on temporary hold pending analyst verification.
- In suspicious cases under Section 12 of the PMLA, a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) must be filed with the Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND) within seven working days.
