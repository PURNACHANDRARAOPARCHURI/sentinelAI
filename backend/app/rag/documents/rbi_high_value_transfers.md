# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Directions on High-Value Transactions & Deficit Protection
## Master Circular - Fraud Risk Management in High-Value Payment Rails (RTGS, NEFT, IMPS)
### Section: Mandatory Enhanced Due Diligence for High-Value & Deficit Transactions

#### 1. High-Value Transaction Thresholds
- All outward fund transfers equal to or exceeding ₹5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees) initiate enhanced scrutiny under Core Banking Fraud Monitoring Frameworks.
- For transfers exceeding ₹10,00,000 (Ten Lakh Rupees) or ultra-high transfers (>= ₹50,00,000), banks must mandate multi-agent behavioral corroboration and step-up verification.
- Automated fast-path straight-through processing (STP) is strictly prohibited for out-of-pattern transfers exceeding ₹5,00,000.

#### 2. Unauthorized Deficit & Overdraft Attempts
- Any transfer request where the debit amount exceeds available ledger balance without pre-approved collateralized overdraft facility represents an unauthorized deficit anomaly.
- Deficit transactions must immediately trigger institutional holds and escalate to Fraud Investigation units for forensic analysis.
- Account-draining patterns leaving zero ledger balance following multi-lakh fund movements represent primary indicators of account takeover (ATO) or mule funneling.
