# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Circular
## Circular No: RBI/2017-18/15 | DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18
### Subject: Customer Protection – Limiting Liability of Customers in Unauthorised Electronic Banking Transactions

#### 1. Zero Liability of a Customer
A customer's entitlement to zero liability shall arise where the unauthorised transaction occurs in the following events:
- Contributory fraud/negligence/deficiency on the part of the bank (irrespective of whether or not the transaction is reported by the customer).
- Third-party breach where the deficiency lies neither with the bank nor with the customer but lies elsewhere in the system, and the customer notifies the bank within three working days of receiving communication from the bank regarding the unauthorised transaction.

#### 2. Limited Liability of a Customer
A customer shall be liable for the loss occurring in the following cases:
- In cases where the loss is due to negligence by a customer, such as where he has shared the payment credentials (PIN, OTP, password), the customer will bear the entire loss until he reports the unauthorised transaction to the bank. Any loss occurring after reporting shall be borne by the bank.
- In cases where the responsibility for the unauthorised electronic banking transaction lies neither with the bank nor with the customer, but lies elsewhere in the system and when there is a delay of four to seven working days after receiving the communication from the bank, the per transaction liability of the customer shall be limited to the transaction value or Rs. 10,000 for Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) accounts and Rs. 25,000 for other accounts, whichever is lower.

#### 3. Mandatory Mechanism for Alert and Reporting
- Banks must ask their customers to mandatorily register for SMS alerts and email alerts for electronic banking transactions.
- The SMS and email alerts must clearly mention the transaction amount, account balance, and provide an immediate mechanism to flag or dispute the transaction.
- On being notified by the customer, the bank must immediately block the impacted payment channel (cards, net banking, UPI) to prevent further unauthorized debits.
